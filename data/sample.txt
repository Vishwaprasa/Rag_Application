1.Obey Traffic Signals: Always stop at red lights, respect stop signs, and proceed only on a clear green signal
2.Wear Seatbelts & Helmets: Ensure drivers, riders, and all passengers wear seatbelts. Motorcyclists and cyclists must wear strapped helmets.
3.Observe Speed Limits: Never exceed the posted speed limit. Adjust your speed downwards in heavy rain or poor visibility.
4.Maintain a Safe Following Distance: Keep a 2 to 3-second gap between your car and the vehicle in front to allow for sudden stops.
5.No Drunk Driving: Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs that impair your motor skills or judgment.
6.Eliminate Distractions: Put away mobile phones and avoid using earphones while walking, cycling, or driving.
7.Use Turn Signals: Always signal your intention to turn or change lanes well in advance so other motorists can react.
8.Yield to Pedestrians: Give right-of-way to pedestrians crossing at designated zebra crossings or intersections.
9.Use Pavements and Crosswalks: Pedestrians should walk on sidewalks. If none exist, walk facing oncoming traffic. Always cross at subways, footbridges, or crosswalks.
10.Do Not Tailgate or Overtake Illegally: Aggressively following too closely or overtaking in blind spots causes severe accidents.
